# CS 262 Team Contract

This contract establishes the expectations, responsibilities, and procedures that all team members agree to follow for the duration of the CS 262 team project.

## 1. Meeting Schedule

- The team will hold a standing meeting every Wednesday, immediately after class, lasting approximately 30 minutes to 1 hour.
- Additional meetings may be scheduled as needed, with reasonable advance notice to all members.
- Members unable to attend a meeting must notify the team in advance and follow up on any decisions or action items they missed.

## 2. Expectations

- Each member is expected to commit 5–6 hours per week to project-related work, including meetings, individual tasks, and collaboration.
- All members are expected to come to meetings prepared, having completed their assigned work or communicated any blockers in advance.
- Members are expected to act professionally and respectfully toward one another at all times.

## 3. Communication

- All project communication will take place through Microsoft Teams.
- Members are expected to respond to messages within 6 hours during reasonable hours.
- Important decisions, deadlines, and updates should be posted in the team channel so all members stay informed, even if discussed elsewhere first.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Project Manager (PM):** Leads team meetings, assigns tasks and roles to team members, and manages project resources.
- **Lead Engineer (LE):** Reviews all pull requests and merge requests before they are merged into the main codebase.
- Roles may be reassigned by team agreement if workload or circumstances change over the course of the project.

## 5. Equitable Contribution & Conflict Resolution

- All members are expected to contribute equitably to the team's work.
- When disagreements or conflicts arise, members will first seek to understand the issue from each other's perspectives before working toward a reasonable, mutually agreeable resolution.
- If a conflict cannot be resolved within the team, members agree to bring the matter to the course instructor or TA for guidance.
